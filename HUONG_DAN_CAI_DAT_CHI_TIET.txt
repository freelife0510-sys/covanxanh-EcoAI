BUOC 1: CAI DAT MOI TRUONG NODE.JS (Chi can lam 1 lan dau tien)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Neu may tinh chua co Node.js, hay truy cap trang chu:
   https://nodejs.org/
2. Tai ban khuyen nghi "LTS" ve may va cai dat (nhan Next -> Next -> Finish).
3. De kiem tra da cai thanh cong chua, ban mo Command Prompt (cmd) va go:
   node -v
   Neu hien ra phien ban (vi du v18.x hoac v20.x, v22.x) la thanh cong!

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BUOC 2: CHAY UNG DUNG (CUC KY DON GIAN)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[CACH 1 - NHANH NHAT CHO WINDOWS]:
- Ban chi can bam dup chuot (double-click) vao file:
  "chay-ecoai-windows.bat"
- He thong se tu dong kiem tra, cai dat thu vien va tu dong mo trinh duyet
  tai dia chi: http://localhost:3000

[CACH 2 - CHAY BANG DONG LENH THU CONG]:
1. Mo thu muc chua ma nguon EcoAI nay.
2. Mo Command Prompt hoac PowerShell tai thu muc:
   (Nhan vao thanh dia chi thu muc trong File Explorer, go "cmd" roi Enter).
3. Cai dat thu vien:
   npm install
4. Khoi dong may chu:
   npm run dev
5. Mo trinh duyet web (Chrome, Edge, Coc Coc) va truy cap:
   http://localhost:3000

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BUOC 3: SU DUNG & KET NOI GOOGLE GEMINI API (Tuy chon)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Ung dung hoat dong day du ca o "Che do Su pham Mo phong Offline" (khong can API key)
